Figure 2. Department of Justice Components and Grant Information

  • Department of Justice
    DOJ’s Main Grantmaking Components
    • OJPOffice of Justice Programs
      • BJA
        Bureau of Justice Assistance

        Funds law enforcement, courts, corrections, treatment, reentry, justice information sharing, and community-based partners.

        Examples

        • Byrne JAG
        • Adult Treatment Court Program
      • BJS
        Bureau of Justice Statistics

        DOJ’s primary statistical agency. Collects and publishes criminal justice data.

        Focus

        • Crime and victimization data
        • Justice system statistics
      • NIJ
        National Institute of Justice

        DOJ’s research, evaluation, and technology agency.

        Focus

        • Evidence and innovation
        • Forensic and school safety research
      • OJJDP
        Office of Juvenile Justice & Delinquency Prevention

        Funds efforts to prevent delinquency and improve juvenile justice systems.

        Examples

        • Title II Formula Grants
        • Youth justice and mentoring programs
      • OVC
        Office for Victims of Crime

        Funds victim compensation and victim assistance.

        Focus

        • Victim services
        • Support for underserved victims
      • SMART
        Office of Sex Offender Sentencing, Monitoring, Apprehending, Registering, and Tracking

        Administers the Sex Offender Registration and Notification Act (SORNA).

        Focus

        • Registry implementation
        • Compliance and notification tools
    • COPSCommunity Oriented Policing Services

      Provides funding for community policing nationwide to reduce crime and build trust between law enforcement and communities.

      Key Areas

      • Law enforcement hiring
      • Community policing development
      • Active shooter training
      • Officer mental health and wellness
    • OVWOffice on Violence Against Women

      Implements the Violence Against Women Act (VAWA) to address domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault, and stalking.

      Key Areas

      • Law enforcement and prosecution strategies
      • Victim services
      • Sexual assault services and family support
